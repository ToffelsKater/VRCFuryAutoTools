What's inside

In editor

Automatic Quest Copy Creator

Add it to an avatar and a copy appears next to it with its own copies of your materials on mobile shaders, PC-only components removed, VRChat constraints and capped copies of big textures. Your originals stay untouched. It keeps the blueprint ID, so switch the SDK to Android and upload it as your Quest version.

At build

Automatic Outfit Toggle Creator

Drop it on an outfit prefab root. Every mesh underneath gets a VRCFury Toggle, laid out in your menu as Outfits/OutfitXY/Top, Outfits/OutfitXY/Jeans and so on.

At build

Automatic Zero Weight Bone Remover

Add it to an avatar or outfit root. Bones no mesh is weighted to are removed at build, cutting bone count and improving your performance rating. PhysBones, constraints and animated bones are left alone.

At build

Automatic Mesh Bone Stripper

List the meshes you actually use. Every bone in their armature that they aren't weighted to is removed at build. Hips, Spine, Chest, Upper Chest, Neck and Head are kept by default, each with its own toggle.

In editor

Automatic PhysBone Collider Creator

Add it to an avatar and every humanoid bone gets a PhysBone collider sized to its length and the mesh around it. On any other bone it covers that bone and all its children. Colliders appear as soon as you add the component, ready for your PhysBones to use.
